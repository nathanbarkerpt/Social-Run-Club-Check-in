Participation at your own risk. Sunday Social Run Club is a casual, community-organised run. By checking in, you confirm you are physically able to take part and that you take part entirely at your own risk. The club, its coaches and volunteers are not liable for any injury, loss or damage that may occur before, during or after a run.

Medical information. You agree to disclose to the coach, before the run starts, any medical condition that could affect your safety or that the coach should be aware of — for example asthma, heart conditions, allergies, diabetes, or anything else relevant in an emergency.

Emergency care. You accept that if the coach or a volunteer believes it is necessary, an ambulance or other emergency services may be called on your behalf, and you consent to this.

Your details. The information you provide (name, phone, email, emergency contact, attendance history) is used to run the club safely — including contacting you in an emergency and tracking attendance — and is kept private, accessible only to club admin.

Marketing communications. By checking in, you also agree that the club may contact you by email or phone with updates, event announcements, and other run club news. You can opt out of these communications at any time by letting the coach know or replying to any message we send you — this won't affect your ability to keep running with the club.

General conduct. Please run within your ability, follow the coach's directions, and look out for fellow runners. The club reserves the right to refuse entry to anyone who does not accept these terms.
